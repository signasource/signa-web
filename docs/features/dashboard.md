Navigation (header pills) lives in `(dashboard)/dashboard/layout.tsx`, next to the SIGNA logo and the organization name.

**Course filter.** Overview, members and purchases can show every contracted course or one of them. Contracted = has a paid purchase: when every purchase of a course is refunded, `signa-api` drops it from the organization, so it leaves `organization.courses` (the picker) and every metric. The choice lives in the URL (`?curso=<courseId>`, `src/features/dashboard/use-course-filter.ts`) so it survives switching sections (nav links keep it) and can be shared; `CourseSelect` renders it, or a plain label when the organization has a single course. An id the organization did not contract is ignored.

**Who counts.** Every section (Resumen, Participantes, the participant detail) uses one rule from `signa-api` (`ParticipantAccessResolver`): a person takes a course when they accepted a seat of a paid purchase of it (even if the access expired or they were removed), or — for people enrolled before seats existed — when the organization enrolled them. A seat whose purchase was refunded gives nothing; an accepted seat can only end up refunded through a Mercado Pago refund or a chargeback, since Signa refuses to refund it. Each person is measured only against the courses they take; with a course filter only who takes that course is listed, and someone who takes no course in scope is not listed at all. Activity (attempts, lessons and modules completed) counts only on courses the person takes. Module % in Contenidos are over who takes that course.

**Period filter (Desde / Hasta).** One period drives every figure of the panel, like a global date picker in analytics tools: `PeriodFilter` (`src/components/dashboard/period-select.tsx`) sits in the header of Participantes, Contenidos and the participant detail (Resumen has no period: its figures are current), next to the course filter. Options: Todo el historial (default), últimos 7 / 30 / 90 días, últimos 12 meses, este mes, mes anterior, and a custom range with two date inputs, validated in `src/lib/date-range.ts` (from ≤ to, to ≤ today, at most 366 days — the API's limit). The period lives in the URL (`?desde=YYYY-MM-DD&hasta=YYYY-MM-DD`, `src/features/dashboard/use-period-filter.ts`); no (or invalid) params mean the whole history. `useWithFilters` keeps course and period on every dashboard link, so switching sections keeps them. What each figure means with a period is decided by `signa-api` ([endpoints](../api/endpoints.md#organizationsapi-srclibapiorganizationsts--mirrors-organizationcontroller)): events count inside it (activos, ejercicios, % correctas, lecciones/módulos completados, tiempo, días activos); states are as of its end (progreso promedio, completaron todo, módulo actual, lo completaron / en curso por módulo). Streaks stay current, so their labels say "hoy" / "actual". Labels that name a rolling window ("últimos 7 días", "últimos 30") switch to "en el período" when one is chosen.

**Seat purchases (organizational business rules).** An organization registers for free and SIGNA validates it before it can buy. Each purchase is one course, a number of seats (one email each) and 5–60 days of access; price depends on the course's sign count, the days and the seats (`PurchasePricing` in signa-api: list price `(6000 + 400 × señas) × (días/30)^0,85`, then 10/15/20/25 % off from 10/25/50/100 seats; e.g. 15 cupos × 30 señas × 30 días = $243.000). Purchases are independent. Payment goes through Mercado Pago Checkout Pro (webhook + the panel's sync), and the confirmation sends a personal invitation per email and opens a 10-day window; the API reminds automatically on day 5. Each employee's days start when they accept on `/invitacion` (log in or register with the invited email). Seat states shown: Esperando el pago, Invitación enviada, Invitación vencida (seat lost), Acceso activo, Acceso vencido (progress kept, lessons locked), Quitado (removing a participant revokes the seat). **Refunds that undo a purchase** (status Reembolsada): the organization's withdrawal within 10 days of paying (the "Anular la compra" form asks for a required reason from the same list as every refund and an optional comment, shown later as "Comentario: «…»"), or — at any time, since the money is gone — a refund made in Mercado Pago or a chargeback. Pending invitations are cancelled, active accesses end like an expiration (progress kept and readable) and everyone affected, the org admins and Signa are emailed. A partial refund or an open dispute changes nothing and only alerts Signa.

## Design vs. API gaps (deliberate deviations from the prototype)

The prototype mocked data the API does not expose. Revisit if `signa-api` adds it:

- **Members list:** no per-filter counts.
- **Period filter:** streaks (`participantsWithStreak`, `longestCurrentStreak`, `currentStreak`) are current only; the API stores no streak history.
- **Member detail:** progress is per course (`MemberCourseProgressResponse`), not per module/lesson; no "recent activity" feed, no per-module accuracy.

## Admin · Compras (`/admin/purchases`)

Design source: Claude Design "Refactorización apartado compras" (`Compras Admin.dc.html`). Backend: `AdminPurchaseController` (unified list/detail) and `AdminRefundRequestController`; client in [../api/endpoints.md](../api/endpoints.md#adminapi-purchases-and-refund-requests-srclibapiadmints).

**Read-only follow-up of payments.** Payments are confirmed by Mercado Pago (webhook) or by the org panel's sync; the back-office **no longer confirms payments by hand** (`confirmPayment` and its panel were removed). It lists, filters and drills into purchases, and handles refund requests.

The page keeps its view state client-side (`{ kind: "list" | "purchase" | "refund" }`). A link `?purchase=<id>&type=SEATS|GEMS|COURSE` (`lib/admin-purchase-link.ts`, used by the metrics' purchase lists) opens that purchase's detail; navigating away drops the params from the URL.

**List view — Compras tab**

- Full-width table of all three purchase types (SEATS · GEMS · COURSE) merged, newest first. Purchases that have a RESOLVED refund request are excluded unless they are REFUNDED — otherwise they appear only in the Reembolsos tab.
- Type segmented control (Todas / Cupos / Gemas / Cursos, with counts on the full dataset).
- Pill filters: Estado (PENDING_PAYMENT / PAID / CANCELLED / REFUNDED), Fecha (Hoy / 7 d / 30 d / Personalizado), Orden (date or amount, asc/desc).
- Free-text search across buyer name, username, email, product, Mercado Pago order id and purchase id; active filter chips with individual remove and "Limpiar todo".
- Pagination in the browser (10 rows/page, only when needed). Rows show a type badge, product + detail, buyer, amount, status pill, the payment method ("Mercado Pago" / "Sin pagar") and a note when `pendingRefundRequest`.

**Accepted seats are never refunded from here.** Resolving a seat refund request (or any admin refund of seats) is refused by the API when an invitee already accepted; the request panel shows "No se puede reembolsar: al menos un invitado ya aceptó su cupo." (`refundResolveErrorMessage` in `src/lib/purchases.ts`).

**List view — Reembolsos tab**

- Card list of refund requests; the tab badge counts the pending ones. Every refunded purchase without a request (seats, gems or courses: withdrawal, direct refund, Mercado Pago, chargeback) is listed too, as a read-only RESOLVED card (`informational: true`) that opens the purchase detail; a purchase with a request appears only through it. Same search + Tipo / Fecha / Orden filters; PENDING / RESOLVED / Todos segmented control. Each card shows reason, truncated message, requester and age or resolution date.

**Purchase detail**

- Stat cards (total, buyer, payment date + method) and a fact table; a refund-request banner (amber while pending, grey once resolved) with "Ver pedido".

**Refund request detail**

- Left: reason ("Sin motivo" when none was picked), the user's message (hidden when empty), requester and the linked purchase (clickable). Right: resolution panel — internal note, "Marcar como resuelto" (PENDING) or "Guardar nota" + "Reabrir" (RESOLVED) through `updateRefundRequest` (`action: "RESOLVE" | "REOPEN"`, or just `note`).

## Admin · Other back-office screens

- **Resumen (`/admin`)** — counters from `adminApi.stats`, pending organizations (first 5), pending sign reports and course versions by status.
- **Organizaciones (`/admin/organizations`)** — tabs Pendientes / Activas / Rechazadas / Suspendidas / Todas ("Todas" excludes suspended, i.e. dadas de baja). Approve, reject, suspend (a reason is mandatory and shown on the suspended org), reactivate (`PATCH status: ACTIVE`, clears the reason) and "Dar de baja" for rejected/suspended orgs (`DELETE`, confirmed inline; frees their emails; 409 if the org has purchases).
- **Reportes de señas (`/admin/sign-reports`)** — individual reports (`adminApi.signReports`) with status tabs (Pendientes by default), reason / sign / date filters; Pending → Revisado → Resuelto / Rechazado with an optional internal note; the API records who resolved it.
- **Usuarios (`/admin/users`)** — Usuarios (search, role filter, **role-aware detail modal** `user-detail-modal.tsx`: enrollments for learners, organization + purchases for org admins, enable/disable and delete), Administradores (`AdminsPanel`) and Lista de espera (`adminApi.waitlist`).
- **Cursos (`/admin/courses`)** — course CMS; a course-version card shows the editor's avatar and name and the enrollment count. The editor can edit `PERFORM_SIGN` signs and check blocks before publishing. See [../routing.md](../routing.md).
- **Señas (`/admin/signs`)** → [admin-signs.md](./admin-signs.md). **Métricas de uso** → [admin-usage-metrics.md](./admin-usage-metrics.md).
